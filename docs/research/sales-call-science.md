# Sales call research and frameworks

Research notes gathered on 2026-09-06 for the Call Drill playbook. Numbers
from Gong and Chorus are vendor-published analyses of their own customers'
call recordings, not peer-reviewed studies, and several pages could not be
fetched in full, so figures come from search snippets. Re-verify before
quoting a number to an agent.

## Talk ratio and listening

- Gong (vendor blog): highest-performing B2B conversations run at about a 43:57 talk-to-listen ratio; average reps talk 65 to 70 percent. https://www.gong.io/blog/talk-to-listen-conversion-ratio
- Gong help docs recommend keeping the rep's longest monologue under 2 minutes 30 seconds. https://help.gong.io/docs/analyze-team-performance
- Gong: winning demos have more speaker switches per minute; interactivity matters most early. https://www.gong.io/blog/sales-demos
- Chorus.ai benchmark (2019, 500K+ discovery calls): top reps at 45 to 52 percent talk time. https://docs.chorus.ai/hc/en-us/articles/360017551373-Benchmarks-The-Perfect-Discovery-Flow
- Chorus.ai: on cold calls that convert, rep talk time is under half (about 44 percent). https://blog.chorus.ai/focus-on-the-questions-not-the-pitch

## Questions

- Gong (519K calls): discovery success peaks at 11 to 14 questions per call, spread through the call, about problems and goals rather than features. https://www.gong.io/blog/nailing-your-sales-discovery-calls
- Chorus.ai: more than two questions per minute reduces engagement; deals with two or more "engaging moments" in discovery are more likely to advance. https://docs.chorus.ai/hc/en-us/articles/360017551373-Benchmarks-The-Perfect-Discovery-Flow
- Chorus.ai: successful cold calls get the prospect asking two to three questions. https://blog.chorus.ai/focus-on-the-questions-not-the-pitch
- SPIN Selling (Neil Rackham, Huthwaite): Situation, Problem, Implication, Need-payoff questions; skilled sellers explore needs in depth before closing. https://www.huthwaiteinternational.com/spin-methodology
- Sandler pain funnel: nested open questions ("Tell me more about that", "Can you give me an example?", "How long has that been a problem?", "What have you tried?", "Did that work?", "How much has that cost you?", "How do you feel about that?"). https://go.sandler.com/stp/insights/blog/categories/sales-process/pain-funnel-questions-for-b2b-sales-the-three-le/
- Gap Selling (Keenan): current state, desired future state, and the gap between them. https://salesgrowth.com/keenan/
- Chris Voss: calibrated "how" and "what" questions give the other side a sense of control. https://www.blackswanltd.com/newsletter/using-calibrated-questions%EF%B8%8F-to-win-more-deals

## Objections and price

- Gong (67K calls): top reps pause about five times longer after an objection (roughly 1.5 seconds versus 0.3) and respond with a question 54 percent of the time versus 31 percent for average reps. Unsuccessful reps interrupt and speed up. https://www.gong.io/blog/handling-sales-objections
- Gong: win rates are highest when price is discussed on the first call, after value is established. https://www.gong.io/blog/data-reveals-the-best-time-to-talk-price-and-budget
- Voss: mirroring (repeat the last one to three words), labelling ("It sounds like..."), and the accusation audit (name their likely objections before they raise them). https://www.blackswanltd.com/newsletter/what-is-the-black-swan-accusation-audit
- Voss: "no-oriented" questions and aiming for "That's right" after a summary. https://www.heartland.us/resources/entrepreneurs-studio/podcast/episode-16-chris-voss-part-2
- Rackham: traditional closing techniques were negatively correlated with success in larger sales. https://www.huthwaiteinternational.com/blog/complete-guide-to-spin-selling

## Closing for the next step

- Gong: reps skip next steps in 26 percent of introductory meetings; close rates fall sharply when next steps are not discussed on the first call. https://www.gong.io/blog/short-sales-cycle
- Gong: the most successful cold calls spend a large share of time on next steps after the meeting is booked. https://www.gong.io/blog/cold-call-stats
- Gong: a specific time-and-date ask books more meetings than an open-ended ask or "still interested?". https://www.gong.io/blog/short-sales-cycle

## Delivery and pace

- Gong (500K+ calls): no relationship between filler words and win rate. Top reps did not use fewer fillers. https://www.gong.io/blog/filler-words-in-sales
- Gong (90K cold calls): "How have you been?" as an opener had a much higher success rate; stating "the reason for my call is..." also raised success. https://www.gong.io/blog/how-to-cold-call
- Voss: the "late-night FM DJ voice", lower and slower and calm. https://www.masterclass.com/classes/chris-voss-teaches-the-art-of-negotiation/chapters/mastering-delivery
- Jacob, Gueguen, Martin and Boulbry (2011, Journal of Retailing and Consumer Services): mimicking customers' verbal behaviour raised sales in person. https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0969698910001165

## Role-play training evidence

- Fu and Li (International Journal of Instruction, 2025): meta-analysis of 12 studies found a large positive effect for role-play teaching, strongest on skills. Not sales-specific. https://www.e-iji.net/dosyalar/iji_2025_1_17.pdf
- Mani, Kothandaraman, Kashyap and Ashnai (Journal of Marketing Education, 2016): success in sales role-play competitions predicted job-interview success. https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0273475315606785
- Medical education: peer role-play and standardized patients deliver comparable communication gains; feedback and reflection are the active ingredients. https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC4619415/
- Vendor claims about AI role-play lifting quota attainment have no citable study behind them.
