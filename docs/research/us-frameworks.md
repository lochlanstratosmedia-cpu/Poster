# US and international real estate call frameworks

Research notes gathered on 2026-09-06 for the Call Drill playbook. Source
pages were blocked from the research sandbox, so lines come from
search-result excerpts of the pages named. Check quotes against the live
page before using them in training material.

## Tom Ferry

- Expired opening: say you noticed the home came off the market and ask if they still want to sell. https://www.tomferry.com/blog/how-to-contact-expired-listings/
- Expired discovery: where they would go, time frame, why they think it did not sell, how they picked the last agent. https://www.tomferry.com/blog/how-to-contact-expired-listings/
- FSBO discovery: where they are moving and how soon, then the reason behind the move, before setting an appointment. https://www.tomferry.com/blog/art-of-real-estate-scripts/
- Open house follow-up: call within 24 to 48 hours, ask what they thought of the home and the price for the area, then whether they are watching values or considering a move in 6 to 12 months. https://www.tomferry.com/blog/mega-open-house-script/
- Listen for motivation, timeline, frustration, and objections before offering an observation. https://www.tomferry.com/blog/approaching-expired-listings-tips/
- Objections in three steps: acknowledge the concern, ask a clarifying question, guide to a logical next step. https://www.tomferry.com/blog/5-scripts-to-overcome-objections/
- "We'll wait": agree it makes sense, then offer pre-marketing as "coming soon" so there is nothing to lose. https://www.tomferry.com/blog/top-10-objections-and-the-scripts-to-overcome-them/
- Price reduction: state that agents, the market, and buyers have rejected the price, then stay quiet until the seller speaks. https://www.tomferry.com/blog/real-estate-pricing-strategy/
- Scripts are a framework, not a robot voice; role play monthly. https://www.tomferry.com/blog/art-of-real-estate-scripts/

## Mike Ferry

- Expired discovery questions: when they plan to interview the right agent for the job, where they would go, what stopped the sale. https://assets-prod.mikeferry.com/main/files/AW_EXPIRED_SCRIPT_OVER_THE_PHONE.pdf
- FSBO discovery: where next, how soon, motivation on a 1 to 10 scale, current marketing, with a short affirmation after each answer. https://assets-prod.mikeferry.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/PS_FSBO_script.pdf
- Pre-qualify every prospect before the appointment: other agents, motivation, timeline, target price, and the commitment question. https://assets-prod.mikeferry.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/08-28-17-Pre-Qualifying-the-Listing-Presentation-Script.pdf
- Use "and" rather than "but" to transition from validating a concern to responding. https://callin.io/mike-ferry-scripts-for-cold-calling/
- Price reduction: weekly update call reporting showings and offers; "I made a mistake on price" framing with options. https://assets-prod.mikeferry.com/main/files/AW_PRICE_REDUCTION_SCRIPT.pdf
- Alternate choice close: "Monday or Tuesday at 4pm?" https://callin.io/mike-ferry-scripts-for-cold-calling/

## Brian Buffini

- Do not read a script verbatim; jot major points, greet, and if they are pressed for time wrap up pleasantly. https://blog.buffini.com/the-lost-art-of-phone-calls/
- Referral ask: "Oh, by the way..." https://blog.buffini.com/ask-clients-referral/

## Ricky Carruth

- Make the prospect comfortable in the first seconds; aim the call at what you can do for them. https://theclose.com/real-estate-cold-calling-scripts/
- Tone, pacing, and authenticity matter more than the exact words. https://www.redx.com/podcast/live-prospecting-expired-listings/

## Kevin Ward

- Expired: when will they interview agents again for the job of actually getting it sold, where were they going, what stopped the sale, how they chose the last agent, how they felt about that agent's work. https://hooquest.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/Expired-Scripts-HOOQUEST.pdf
- Never make the seller wrong. https://medium.com/@kevinward/fsbo-objection-bring-me-a-buyer-9e4f3f0b6576
- Scripts are a tool; internalize them by training like a professional. https://remindermedia.com/podcast/ep-195-why-sales-scripts-are-bs-with-kevin-ward/

## Jeb Blount

- Five-step opening: name, identify yourself, reason for calling, a "because" tied to their situation, ask and stop talking. https://www.mentorist.app/action/master-the-art-of-telephone-pr_537/
- Ask with confidence and assume yes, then be quiet; be ready for reflex responses, brush-offs, and objections. https://www.insidesales.com/secrets-of-phone-prospecting-jeb-blount/
- Ledge, Disrupt, Ask: a memorised "ledge" response buys the thinking brain a moment. https://salesgravy.com/how-to-use-the-ledge-technique-for-sales-objection-handling-ask-jeb/

- Call the database just to connect, no pitch. https://curbhe.ro/real-estate-ninja/

## Ten teachings that recurred most

1. Ask motivation, destination, and timeline early.
2. Acknowledge before answering an objection.
3. Ask a clarifying or isolating question before responding.
4. Never make the prospect wrong.
5. Ask for the appointment directly, often with an alternate choice.
6. Then stop talking.
7. Give a reason for the call tied to their situation.
8. Pre-qualify every prospect before the appointment.
9. Frame price reductions with market data and showing counts, not opinion.
10. Internalise scripts through practice so they sound natural.
