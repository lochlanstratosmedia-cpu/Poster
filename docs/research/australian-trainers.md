# Australian real estate call trainers

Research notes gathered on 2026-09-06 for the Call Drill playbook. Most
trainer sites were blocked from the research sandbox, so lines come from
search-result excerpts of the pages named. Treat quoted lines as excerpts,
not verified against the full page.

## Josh Phegan

- Warm neighbour call: name the awkwardness, then give news about a nearby listing and invite them to the open. https://joshphegan.com.au/news/where-to-start-with-prospecting
- Cold call: aim for an opt-in, not a listing. Offer the brochure, invite them to the open, ask permission to tell them what it sells for, then call back at "sold" and ask for an appraisal. https://help.joshphegan.com.au/en/articles/3582174-tips-for-cold-calling-and-door-knocking
- Appraisal offer framed as a small free service so the owner can make better decisions, ending with "would that be ok?". https://joshphegan.com.au/free-training/scripts-and-dialogues
- Focus discovery on why the client is coming to market, not how or when. https://eliteagent.com/managing-your-pipeline-josh-phegan/
- Open home follow-up: call the next day and offer a second look; if keen, book a private buyer appointment tomorrow rather than the next open. https://joshphegan.com.au/media/repricing-and-the-art-of-price-reductions
- Objections: first sort a real objection from a delay tactic. Use trial closes through the presentation. For fee, the "Why, If I, Will You, Why" sequence. https://www.realestatebusiness.com.au/sales/7016-josh-phegan-how-to-easily-overcome-objections
- Fee: "there can only be one cheapest agent", so compete on value. https://eliteagent.com/theres-only-ever-one-cheapest-agent-josh-phegan/
- Price reduction: if marketing and vendor reporting were done as promised, the conversation is simple: the market is not responding, so sharpen the price to attract buyers the agent can then negotiate up. https://joshphegan.com.au/free-training/price-reductions

## Tom Panos

- "You do not prospect to get a listing, you prospect to get contacts." Always be educating rather than always closing. https://eliteagent.com/tom-panos-how-agents-win-market-share-in-a-downturn/
- The "gentle interruption" needs a strong "because" for the call. https://tompanos.com.au/the-best-prospecting-scripts/
- Database follow-up: mention buyer interest in the area at a price band and ask if they want an updated appraisal; if not, offer a short market snapshot of comparable sales. http://realtair.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Tom-Panos_Realtair_PRF_Scripts-and-Dialogues_2023.pdf
- "What would you do differently": inspect rival open homes and bring the notes; "when your home comes on the market you will be in competition, not in isolation." https://www.realestatebusiness.com.au/industry/9892-tom-panos-reveals-clever-listing-presentation-tactic
- "The other agent has sold more": "We might not sell the most homes, but I pride on selling the homes for most." https://tompanos.com.au/script-dialogue-the-other-agent-has-sold-more-why-should-we-list-with-you/
- "It's the process not the promise of a price that will get you top dollar." https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/tom-panos-10-powerful-scripts-use-listing-steven-carroll
- "Meet the market" is context, not spin; explain changeover figure versus sale figure. https://tompanos.com.au/3-scripts-dialogues-for-this-changing-market/
- Trust is built by frequency, so the listing is won before the presentation. https://eliteagent.com/transform-2017-week-8-tom-panos-get-appraisal-win-listing/

## Lee Woodward

- Qualify the incoming buyer enquiry call first; "negotiation starts from the very first time you make contact." https://leewoodward.com.au/article/buyer-management-you-will-never-remember-them-all-lee-woodward
- Vendors: "24 winning questions and the 25th is the closer"; make the presentation a chat, but a premeditated one; listings are lost through "inability to sell the feeling." https://leewoodward.com.au/article/winning-the-business-lee-woodward
- A five-step "go-forward plan" as the close (agreement, sales team inspection, photos, solicitor details). https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/closing-lee-woodward-john-mcgrath-heidi-campbell

## John McGrath

- Inbound seller call: offer a fixed slot tomorrow, then gather information first to make the best use of their time: how long owned, improvements, whether both owners will be present. https://leewoodward.com.au/article/john-mcgrath-building-the-right-listing-presentation-for-you
- Regular, honest communication backed by quality information makes getting the right price easy. https://leewoodward.com.au/article/john-mcgrath-getting-the-price-right

## Aaron Shiner

- Three-step dialogue to sort immediate sellers, long-term "equity check" prospects, and database adds. Handle "I'm not selling" with value rather than argument. "You can't list what you didn't appraise." https://www.aaronshiner.com.au/blog/the-prospecting-script-every-real-estate-agent-needs-this-spring
- Short fixed buyer qualifying question sets kept by the phone. https://www.aaronshiner.com.au/blog/the-7-essential-buyer-qualifying-questions
- "We'll wait until next year": a two-stage selling system where stage one is quiet pre-marketing to active premium buyers, so the seller can get a result before the open market. https://www.aaronshiner.com.au/blog/the-simple-dialogue-that-turns-a-not-until-next-year-into-a-yes
- Vendor management: at least four substantive vendor conversations a week covering enquiry, inspections, offers, buyer feedback, days on market, comparables, and a clear recommendation. https://www.aaronshiner.com.au/blog/the-vendor-management-script-every-real-estate-agent-needs-in-a-shifting-market

## Ray White and others

- Ray White call days: lead with a recent nearby sale, say it has affected their value, and offer an appraisal. Pre-warm contacts, pick an icebreaker, rehearse. https://eliteagent.com/ray-white-members-hit-the-phones-for-fast-paced-appraisal-drive/ and https://eliteagent.com/appraisal-playbook-10-lessons-in-securing-leads-making-connections-and-building-your-pipeline/
- Mat Steinwede: if "not selling", reply that it has nothing to do with selling, it is information about the area. https://leewoodward.com.au/article/mat-steinwede-the-real-estate-system-promoting-you
- Claudio Encina: fee objection handled with a "good faith guarantee"; avoid pushy selling. https://eliteagent.com/the-wisdom-of-claudio-encina-30-lessons-from-the-coach-who-still-has-a-coach/
- Client Collective: "neighbour in your street" appraisal call. https://clientcollective.com.au/scripts/appraisal-inspiration-the-neighbour-in-your-street/
- MRI Software AU: listen, acknowledge, pivot with a question or value-add; "I already have an agent" answered with an offer of a market update as a benchmark. https://www.mrisoftware.com/au/blog/real-estate-cold-calling-scripts/

## Ten teachings that came up most often

1. Have a reason for the call (nearby sale, new listing, market update).
2. Offer the appraisal as a free, low-pressure service.
3. Prospect for contacts and relationships, not listings.
4. Handle "not selling" by offering information instead of arguing.
5. Qualify buyers early with a short fixed question set.
6. Ask why the client is moving, not just when.
7. Follow up open home visitors the next day with a second look or private appointment.
8. Separate real objections from delay tactics and isolate with trial closes.
9. The fee objection is a value conversation.
10. Price reductions rest on constant factual vendor reporting.
